# Open Source Policy

ApuLab Station is an open-source and open-content project.

## Licensing model

The repository uses a license split based on the type of material:

- **Software source code** is licensed under the **MIT License**. See [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).
- **Original non-code content and assets** are licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**. See [`CONTENT-LICENSE.md`](CONTENT-LICENSE.md).
- **Third-party materials** remain under their original licenses. See [`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## What this allows

You may study, fork, modify, redistribute, and build on the software under the MIT License.

You may share and adapt original ApuLab Station creative and educational content, including for commercial use, under CC BY 4.0, provided the required attribution is preserved.

## Brand and attribution

Copyright licensing and trademark rights are separate. Copyrightable logo and identity artwork included in this repository is covered by CC BY 4.0 unless otherwise marked, but the licenses do not grant a right to imply official endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation with ApuLab Station.

See [`BRAND_POLICY.md`](BRAND_POLICY.md) for the distinction between open content and brand use.

## Research data and credentials

Participant credentials, secrets, personal information, private datasets, and non-public research records are not open-source repository content and must not be committed to version control.

Contributions are welcome. By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the license applicable to the part of the repository you modify.
